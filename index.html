Welcome to Chromatica

Discover new colors, build your collection, and unlock all 20 chapters.

  1. Generate: create a random color by blending two or more colors you have already found.
  2. Collect: every new color is added to your collection. Select any swatch to see its name and hex code.
  3. Combine: select two or more found colors in the right panel, then press Combine colors to mix them yourself.
  4. Rebirth: when you meet the chapter's unique-color goal, press Rebirth to unlock the next chapter. Your collection is never reset.
  5. Chapter 12: duplicate colors can appear from this chapter onward, making discovery less predictable. Duplicates do not count toward the chapter goal.

Start with the primary colors—red, yellow, and blue—plus white, gray, and black. Generated shades are named after the closest entry in this included Coolors-inspired color-name set. Browse the Coolors Color Library for the full library.
